The user-provided originals are preserved alongside generated cutouts. The ESP32, breadboard, HC-SR04, DHT11, and M-F jumper wire listings use transparent PNGs so their photo backgrounds do not show as white rectangles. The HC-SR04 cutout preserves all four sensor pins.

Several catalog photos are representative rather than exact stock photos: the jumper-wire variants share the available M-M wire photo; the LED and resistor listings share a general component assortment; the potentiometer photo shows a standard rotary part rather than the listed 3362P trimmers; and the JST-XH and starter-kit photos show an adapter and a different kit, respectively. The active-buzzer photo shows the buzzer component rather than its module board.

The LM2596 module, tactile-button pack, and pin-header strip still use their fallback icons because no suitable locally downloadable, openly licensed product photo was available. Replace these with store-owned photos when possible.
